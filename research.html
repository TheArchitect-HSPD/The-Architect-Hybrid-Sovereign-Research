The October 2026 addendum explicitly calls for testing beyond one conversational model. The programme below tracks that documentary roadmap and labels unimplemented activities as planned.

CORE QUESTION

Does independent oversight improve evidentiary reliability?

Compare the Arbiter concept against clear baselines using hidden ground truth, independent processes and explicit failure conditions.

RESEARCH QUESTION

False agreement

Can a sovereign evidence reviewer identify unsupported or mistaken claims when agent consensus is contaminated by correlated inputs?

EPISTEMIC HAZARD

Common-origin evidence

Multiple agent opinions may be downstream of a single faulty source; independence must be demonstrated, not presumed.

FAILURE CONDITION

Unjustified certainty

Record instances where a conclusion is confidently treated as established despite absent or contradictory source support.

PROJECT ROADMAP

Source-derived validation phases

These stages are drawn from the addendum §9. Their presentation here does not mean any stage has been independently completed.

01

Deterministic test harness

Reproducible state engine, seeded events, logged actions, provenance graphs, causal registers, resource budgets and exact replay.

Status: proposed / not externally validated
02

Multi-instance replication

Run separated agent processes and test whether conclusions still stand when upstream information sources and models vary.

Status: required next stage
03

Adversarial replication

Pre-register failure thresholds and invite external adversarial reviewers, rather than allowing internal simulated agreement to stand as proof.

Status: independent partners not yet documented
04

Report failures and revisions

Publish limitations and negative results; make patches distinguishable from independent evidence of success.

Status: disclosure standard proposed

Status recorded for this public presentation, 26 October 2026.

Important research discipline

A valid result could demonstrate that The Arbiter is ineffective, unreliable, or creates a new epistemic bottleneck. Such a finding would still be valuable and must be reported faithfully.
