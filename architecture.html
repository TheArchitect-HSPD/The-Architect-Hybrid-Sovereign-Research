Architecture

Twenty guardians.
One sovereign Arbiter.

The 20 functional positions were proposed in the October 2026 HSPD agent discussion. Their heroic-fantasy names are newer, provisional design labels; no role receives additional authority merely through a name.

Two rosters, not one

The 20-position architecture shown here is distinct from the ten simulated role labels used in HSPD Arena 0.1. That historical group was Anchor, Anvil, Blackbox, Flora, Genome, Horizon, Kade, Lovely, Mira and Spark, all generated by one underlying model.
