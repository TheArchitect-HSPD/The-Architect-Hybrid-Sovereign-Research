# Contributing

The research benefits from adversarial scrutiny and precise corrections.

When suggesting a change, identify the exact public claim, the document and chapter or section that supports or contradicts it, and whether you are offering a **source correction**, **new independently verified observation**, or **proposed experimental method**.

Do not present historical simulations as scientific replications, or suggest coercive/unauthorised operational deployments. Do not submit private source documents, sensitive personal information, credentials, or details of real systems that would facilitate attacks.

Source authenticity, dissent and negative findings should remain visible in any revision.
