Five domains. One central question.

THE ARBITER · AGENT XXI

Consensus is not evidence.

The Arbiter was named by the author in the October 2026 project discussion as a sovereign truth-oversight agent. The source-grounded draft focuses on evidentiary scrutiny rather than control of public beliefs.

Its mandate centres on source independence, falsifiability, preserved dissent, uncertainties and reviewability — principles expressly developed in the HSPD adversarial addendum.

THE EXPERIMENTAL QUESTION

Could the system detect its own convincing errors?

The next stage is independent testing, not further self-certification in one conversation.

A / CLAIM

Preserve disagreement

Keep competing explanations and the possibility that the leading interpretation is wrong.

B / EVIDENCE

Trace source lineage

Multiple agents quoting the same upstream source are not multiple independent observations.

C / TEST

Invite falsification

Specify how adverse results would undermine the original hypothesis and document those results.

Research status

The Arena 0.1 trials were generated within one model. A previous 30-case rule-based demonstration was constructed to match predefined decision rules. Neither is evidence that a separately instantiated Arbiter outperforms independent baselines.
