NON-NEGOTIABLE DISTINCTIONS

Statements this site does not make

No fabricated endorsements

Role-played “verdicts” attributed to institutions in historical synthetic scenarios must not be represented as institutional reviews or endorsements.

No invented benchmark success

Internal simulation percentages are not validated real-world peace or reliability metrics; the earlier rule-based exercise was not a blind independent test.

No invented operational agents

Twenty-one named positions describe a proposed architecture. Their names do not imply running processes, credentials, model separation, or tools.

No unexamined ideological authority

The book’s boundary on truth/justice/ideology must be reconciled explicitly with The Arbiter’s independent epistemic role.
