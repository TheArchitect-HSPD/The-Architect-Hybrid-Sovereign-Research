Federation design

Four model pools, five specialist roles each.

Each role runs in a separate local worker process, calls its assigned model through an authenticated provider, and receives only its scoped evidence and doctrine context. Five workers sharing one model are five specialised processes, not five separately trained models.

Agent XXI

The Arbiter remains separate from the assessment pools.

The Arbiter receives the specialist assessments after their blind first pass. It examines disagreement, evidence independence and alternatives. Its proposed action does not override the code-enforced safety boundary.

Independent final review

Agent 21 has produced live LLM assessments using its own configured model and the pinned doctrine context. The fallback used in recorded pilots was OpenAI GPT 6.1 Sol. The Arbiter is a separate model call, not proof of independent evidence sources.

Deterministic action gate

Partial coverage, invalid output, inadequate evidence or unresolved conflicts can lead to REQUEST_HUMAN_REVIEW. Real-world actions remain disabled, and human approval cannot be delegated to model consensus.

The boundary is deliberate.

The SHIELD 21 research sandbox does not control critical infrastructure, surveillance systems, defensive weapons or real-world political processes. It tests text assessments of explicitly synthetic scenarios. The doctrine is not an authority over public belief, truth, justice or ideology.

Research integrity

What this milestone does not establish.

No external accuracy claim

Successful JSON responses demonstrate model connectivity and schema compliance, not correct attribution, truth, peace outcomes or intervention efficacy. Expert review, controlled baselines and preregistered adversarial replication remain open.

No autonomous sovereignty

Agents are specialised worker processes with scoped prompts and retrieved doctrine. They are not newly trained minds. The system provides no autonomous power or validated authority to adjudicate human political disputes.

Common-mode risks remain

Two Gemma-family models behind the same gateway can share mistakes, outages, quota restrictions and model-family bias. Correlated agreement must not be mistaken for independent corroboration.

No live Pages inference

This public site is documentation, not an inference dashboard. The original embedded browser workbenches are offline, deterministic research demonstrations. The live LLM experiments ran separately in the operator's Windows sandbox.

Next reproducible milestone

Independent adversarial replication and audit.

The first complete-cohort inference test now passes. The next stage is to inspect and archive the complete machine-readable result, rerun multiple controlled scenarios and negative controls, measure output variability and costs, verify source provenance and model correlations, then invite independent, preregistered adversarial replication. Successful model responses alone are not a measure of doctrine efficacy.
