# The Architect — Twenty-Agent Doctrinal Capability Audit (v0.4)

**Status:** Source-grounded research specification, not deployed AI and not author-approved doctrine amendments.

**Evidence base:** *The Hybrid Sovereign* (A), *HSPD / The Architect World Peace Doctrine and Strategy* (B), and *HSPD Adversarial Validation & Errata Addendum v1.1* (C). Full text in `arbiter/knowledge.js`.

## Method and caution

Each proposed agent inherits an existing functional role from the prior 20-agent architecture. Its heroic identity is a subsequent naming proposal. The author's manuscripts provide themes, operational claims and constraints, not 160 previously specified running software skills. The capabilities and tests below are **engineering proposals** linked to exact source passages for critical review. A source keyword match is not a validated mandate. Analyst and oversight roles are rooted especially in the **post-publication** addendum, rather than falsely described as printed in the original books.

The doctrine's non-negotiable boundary (Book I, Chapter 1 §13.8) forbids the system from becoming the final authority over truth, justice and ideology. The Arbiter is restricted to evidence-integrity review; no proposed specialist may launch coercive actions or self-certify.
