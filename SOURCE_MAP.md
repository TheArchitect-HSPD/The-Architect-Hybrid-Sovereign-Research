## Newly invented engineering choices in this version

- Fictional medical-supply corridor claim, six fixtures, confidence scores, root labels, severity labels and known toy ground-truth labels.
- Comparison implementations (raw majority, confidence-only, candidate lineage-aware policy).
- Arbiter numeric threshold: ≥2 supporting observed roots and ≥67% supporting share. **Not doctrinally mandated, not validated.**
- Browser interface, Python CLI, JSON schema, agent trace messages, replay seed handling and metrics.
- All twenty-one role classes are deterministic software roles/context stubs, not simulated independent LLM reasoning or deployed infrastructure.

## Limits of the present artifact

The prototype is meant to make HSPD's proposed evidence checks *auditable*, and to highlight counterexamples. It is not evidence that the doctrine as a whole is feasible, that agents preserve society under real geopolitical threats, or that The Arbiter can identify authentic source independence without a trusted provenance system.
