Published progress

TRIAL 001 completed, 11 November 2026: The operator reports 30 of 30 synthetic cases executed. Twenty-four cases returned all six accepted LLM receipts and six returned five of six. Total accepted receipts: 174/180 (96.67%). Full six-role completion: 24/30 (80%). Incomplete cases: 05 (agent 04), 08 (agent 01), 13 (agent 02), 19 (agent 04), 24 (agent 05), and 27 (agent 01). These are first-attempt integration statistics, not doctrine accuracy, civilian-harm reduction, or peace outcomes. The full local JSON results have not been independently inspected.

Research boundary

What is supported

The operator reported that 30 cases were executed, 24 with six valid receipts and six with only five. The full original JSON reports have not been independently inspected.

What is not supported

All 30 cases have operator-reported results, including six incomplete cases. The underlying full JSON reports have not been independently inspected here.

What the next study requires

Blind held-out cases, matched non-HSPD baselines, independently scored outcome measures, safe ablations, repeated runs and documented uncertainty.
